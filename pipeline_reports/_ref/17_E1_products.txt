  If Cannazon's non-entrants went quiet entirely while Cryptonia's stayed

  that one population was more mobile -- it is that one had somewhere to
  go and the other did not.

==============================================================================
5. What this did NOT test
==============================================================================
  The product-overlap explanation -- Cryptonia was a general market and
  Apollon was general, so its sellers could redeploy, while Cannazon was a
  cannabis specialist with nowhere comparable to go -- is consistent with
  everything above and is NOT tested by any of it. The Kilos table has no
  category column; `price` is a weak and confounded stand-in for product
  type, and a price band that both markets share says only that both sold
  things at similar prices.
