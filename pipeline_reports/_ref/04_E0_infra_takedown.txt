10. VERDICT -- what E0 establishes, and what it does not
==============================================================================
  ESTABLISHED (within the observation window, with n stated):
    * median time online after listing: 1.36 days (32.7 h), n=36262, 93.2% events
    * S(1d)=0.606  S(7d)=0.157  S(30d)=0.050 -- fast for most, a long tail
    * group contrasts are tested above by log-rank and jointly by Cox,
      with the proportional-hazards assumption checked rather than assumed.
  NOT ESTABLISHED:
    * absolute survival -- durations are lower bounds (last sighting, not
      takedown).
    * long-run persistence -- the feed's retention window forbids it (C-29).
    * causality -- these are associations among co-observed URL features.
    * `threat`-type contrasts -- the cohort holds 1 threat value(s), so
      payload and family are substituted via a stated tag rule.
