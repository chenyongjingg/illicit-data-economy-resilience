3. Reading
==============================================================================
  A design whose real beta sits at the null's median has produced no
  evidence of an event effect: the same estimator, on pre-period data
  with no event, returns that number routinely. A design whose real
  beta sits in the tail has produced evidence, provided the null's own
  mean is reported -- a null mean far from 0 means the contrast carries
  a drift that must be explained rather than assumed away.
