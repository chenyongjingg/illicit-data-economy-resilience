At Delta = 1 the label is literally 1[num_timesteps_appeared_in >= 2] and that column is in this matrix.
  The plan names an 'MLP 特征基线'. Built the plan's way -- from the provided table -- it is this. The plan's own risk table fires a fallback ('降级为 GraphSAGE + 手工时序特征') when the temporal GNN improves little over the baseline; against this baseline every honest arm would look weak, so the fallback would fire on an artefact of the baseline rather than on a property of the GNN. That is why `mlp_feature_only` above is rebuilt from the edge stream truncated at t0 instead.
