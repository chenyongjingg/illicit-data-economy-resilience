2. PSM: propensity score and 1:1 nearest-neighbour matching
==============================================================================
  The first attempt matched ZERO pairs, and the reason is substantive
  rather than numerical, so it is measured here instead of worked around.
  `n_markets` has SMD +3.31 because treatment is DEFINED as 'active in
  the target AND in a dead market', which forces n_markets >= 2 for every
  treated seller. A covariate implied by the treatment definition makes
  treatment perfectly predictable and destroys overlap. Four covariate
  sets are tried and the overlap of each is reported.

  covariate set                     n_cov   max|b|  overlap    pairs worstSMD
  A full (as pre-registered)            9    20.61    0.000        0    3.313
  B minus definitional n_markets        8     2.14    0.696       77    1.596
  C Apollon activity + quality          6     2.17    0.886       74    1.596
  D baseline size + quality only        4     2.71    0.949      121    0.519

  ★ best set: D baseline size + quality only -> 121 pairs (76.6% of 158 treated)
